# Outline Report: Batch G19

### Written IDs (4)
- `place-great-mosque-of-cordoba` (Great Mosque of Cordoba)
- `place-deir-el-bahari-temple-of-hatshepsut` (Mortuary Temple of Hatshepsut, Deir el-Bahari)
- `place-gol-gumbaz-bijapur` (Gol Gumbaz, Bijapur)
- `place-ananda-temple-bagan` (Ananda Temple, Bagan)

### Skipped IDs (0)
None. None of the four target sites were previously covered by existing place records (only the person record for Hatshepsut and the broad plain record for Bagan existed). All four sites had at least two independent opened sources confirming dates and coordinates, and all passed `gate.mjs` and `spotcheck.mjs`.

### Least Sure Of
- **Cordoba:** The exact start year of the foundational mosque under Abd al-Rahman I (Cabildo cites 786–788 CE; other chronologies cite 785/786 CE).
- **Hatshepsut:** Divergence between high and low Egyptian New Kingdom chronologies for Hatshepsut's regnal dates (Ministry cites c. 1473–1458 BCE; others cite c. 1479–1458 BCE).
- **Gol Gumbaz:** The exact span of construction prior to completion in 1659 CE during or after Muhammad Adil Shah II's reign (reigned 1627–1657 CE).
- **Ananda Temple:** Dating of completion between c. 1105 CE and c. 1112 CE at the end of Kyanzittha's reign.
