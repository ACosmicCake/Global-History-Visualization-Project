# Seed-list guidance (spec section 3)

Research pointers for each seed target. They are starting points, not facts: verify everything against sources you open, and drop or correct anything that doesn't hold. Kind, scale and importance are suggestions; justify any change.

## Group 2 — Human origins

### -300ka-rise-of-homo-sapiens
Process, human_origins, imp 5. Jebel Irhoud, Morocco (~315 ± 34 ka; Hublin et al. 2017, Richter et al. 2017 Nature); Omo Kibish (Omo I ≥ 233 ka; Vidal et al. 2022 Nature); Florisbad; pan-African (structured within-Africa) origin versus single-region origin as perspectives. `regions` geometry with sourced site coordinates; historical_geography_source on the environment at the time. Sources: nature.com, PMC, Smithsonian Human Origins, NHM London, MPI-EVA.

### -70ka-out-of-africa
Process, human_origins/prehistory, imp 5. Main dispersal ~70–50 ka ancestral to all non-Africans (genetic dating); earlier dispersals (Misliya ~180 ka; Apidima ~210 ka, contested; Skhul/Qafzeh ~100–130 ka); southern (Bab el-Mandeb) versus northern (Sinai/Levant) routes; interbreeding with Neanderthals and Denisovans; arrival in Australia (Madjedbebe ~65 ka versus ~50 ka, contested). `path` or `regions` geometry with dated, sourced waypoints; `path` template; perspectives for dates and routes. Favour Asia, Australia and Pacific targets in the inbox.

### -40ka-extinction-of-neanderthals
Process, human_origins, imp 4. Disappearance ~41–39 ka cal BP (Higham et al. 2014 Nature); claimed late survivals (Iberia, Gorham's Cave; contested); overlap with H. sapiens; ~1–2% Neanderthal DNA in non-Africans (Green 2010, Prüfer); competing explanations (competition, small populations, climate, assimilation) as perspectives. Keep cal BP in as_recorded (BP = before 1950 CE). Inbox ideas: species records for H. neanderthalensis and Denisovans.

### -23ka-peopling-of-the-americas
Process, prehistory, imp 5. Beringian standstill; White Sands footprints (~23–21 ka; Bennett et al. 2021 Science, Pigati et al. 2023, critics); Monte Verde (~14.5 ka); Clovis-first and its abandonment; Pacific coast versus ice-free corridor; genetics (Anzick-1). Strongly contested: perspectives with holders. Indigenous origin accounts may be recorded respectfully as beliefs where sourced. Dated `path`/`regions` waypoints. Sources: science.org, nature.com, PMC, USGS, NPS.

## Group 3 — First civilizations
